How Xbox Grid Sync Works

Quick guide to upgrading and permanently protecting your artwork
The 3-Step Workflow
1
Auto-Discovery & Selection

Directly mirrors your Xbox PC App collection without crawling your hard drives. On initial scan, the app automatically finds and pre-selects the best 1:1 square artwork from SteamGridDB and Steam.

2
Review or Customize

All games show a Ready to Sync indicator. Click any game card to open the Studio, search community alternatives, or drag-and-drop custom images.

3
1-Click Push to Xbox

Click Sync All Artwork to inject all high-resolution square covers directly into your Xbox App folder with automatic original backups.

Protecting Against Xbox App Update Wipes Choose either option below

Microsoft Store updates periodically wipe the Xbox App's thumbnail cache. Xbox Grid Sync provides two ways to stay permanently protected:

Recommended
Option 1: Live System Tray Shield

Leave Xbox Grid Sync minimized to your Windows System Tray. The built-in FileSystemWatcher continuously guards the Xbox App folder and silently restores your covers within 3 seconds whenever an update resets them.

• Instant 3-second recovery • Ultra-low RAM footprint (<40MB)
Headless / No App Running
Option 2: Windows Task Scheduler

Enable the Task Scheduler in Settings. Windows registers an automated task triggered on Event ID 854 (Xbox App update) and User Logon that runs silently in the background (--restore-silent) without keeping the app open.

• Zero background tray processes • Triggers automatically on Windows events
